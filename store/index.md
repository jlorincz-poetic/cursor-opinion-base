# Opinion index

Router for `coding-opinions`. One general principle per line. Open at most 1–2 matching `topics/<slug>.md` files. Do not treat unread rows as defaults or as a checklist.

Statuses: `candidate` (do not treat as default) · `working` (use as default) · `tension` (present both sides) · `retired`
Scope: `domain` is tagged out of defaults (status cell `candidate · domain`). Missing scope is general.
